Work actions

Actions on research output. Public actions help readers cite, save, and reach files; backoffice actions help researchers, curators, and reviewers act on the record's workflow state.

Action Principles

The card action answers one question: what can this person do with this record now? One card has at most one primary action. Unavailable actions are absent, not disabled.

Rule Public Backoffice
Primary action Access CTA on detail headers; card access CTA only in the future v2 pattern. Review, Continue, Edit, Edit & resubmit, Request changes, or View, depending on role and state.
More actions Not used on public cards. Ghost three-dot button, placed directly left of the main action when it exists.
Secondary links Cite and Add to list in the card header. Curator/reviewer utility links in the card footer: files, public page, DOI, WoS, PubMed, Altmetric.
Element choice Actions that navigate are links. Actions that act on the current page are buttons. Every accessible name includes the record title.
