Work card

The primary list unit for research output. Public cards are the minimum readable form; backoffice cards extend the same grammar with workflow state, responsibility, administrative metadata, and actions.

Variants by surface and audience

The regions stay the same; the information payload changes with the surface and the person doing the work.

Footer utilities None Optional Files, public page, and external services

A retraction remains visible on every surface: the record stays public and citable, but its condition is unmistakable.

See work actions for action placement by role and state. Reference-line composition is documented in docs/wip/WORK-CARD-REFERENCE-STYLES.md.

States, access, and actions

Backoffice cards keep four facts distinct: deposit status says where the work is in the workflow; record visibility says whether the record appears publicly; file access says whether the work can be read; record condition, such as retraction, changes how it is trusted. Render each separately.

Backoffice cards

Backoffice cards extend the same card. They add Raven status and visibility, denser scan lines, responsibility blocks, projects, related-record counts, audit trail, and role-specific actions. Journal articles, datasets, and software need golden examples because the shared grammar stays stable while the scan line and metadata payload change by type.
