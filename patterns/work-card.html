Work card

The primary unit for displaying research output in list and search contexts. Three variants: backoffice curator, backoffice researcher, and public. The card uses Bootstrap's card component as a structural base, with surface tokens and a small set of custom classes for domain-specific content.

All metadata visible: status, authors, affiliations, departments, projects, Biblio message, audit trail, VABB, quick links.

Card grammar — region by region

Every work card is the same five regions: __meta (access + type), __actions, __title, __authors (of __author spans), and __pub (the reference line, composed per WORK-CARD-REFERENCE-STYLES). Structural wrappers stay Bootstrap (card-header, card-body).

The title element is surface- and outline-dependent: <h2> on public search results, <p> on backoffice lists, deeper heading levels where the page outline requires (project detail uses <h4> under year <h3>s). The __title class is purely visual — SCSS requires no specific tag.

Access CTA states (Download, Log in, Select file, embargo text) live at Work actions.

__meta — access is always a badge, type is a meta item

Open access Journal article
Restricted access Book chapter
Embargo Doctoral thesis
Lecture Second text item

No files, or all files private, means no access element at all — private leaves no public trace. The separator renders only between consecutive meta items, never after the last.

__actions — Cite and Add to list on every card (v1)

__author — icons outside the link, hidden text inside it

Karen De Pauw (UGent, ORCID 0000-0002-1234-5678), Jonas Maes (UGent), Manuel Esperon-Rodriguez et al.

Authors are prose: comma text nodes between __author spans, up to ten names then et al. Icons sit inside the span but outside the <a> — spacing comes from CSS, and the hover underline stays off the gap. The visually-hidden identifier text inside the link carries what the icons show.

__pub — the reference line, per work type

(2024) European Journal of Crime, Criminal Law and Criminal Justice, 32(1), pp. 58–79.

(2024) in Handbook of urban ecology. London: Routledge, pp. 100–120.

(2024) PhD thesis. Ghent University.

(2026)

Harvard author-date, card variant: the year opens the line (authors and title live above), container titles in <cite>, linked parts keep their search links, datasets carry the year only. Full per-type composition: docs/WORK-CARD-REFERENCE-STYLES.md.
