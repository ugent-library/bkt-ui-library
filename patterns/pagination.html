Accessibility

PartContract
NavigationName the entity in the list. Add “top” and “bottom” when one list repeats its pagination.
Top result countKeep it beside, not inside, the <nav> and add aria-live="polite".
Repeated lower countRepeat the visible text without another live region.
Current pageKeep its href and add aria-current="page". The visible number is its name.
Previous and nextName the links “Previous page” and “Next page”; hide their icons. Use <span class="page-link"> when inert.
GapsUse an inert page item and hide it from assistive technology.
Page size and sortGive each select a label, even when it is visually hidden.

Composition rules

  • Show first, current ±1, and last, with inert gaps between ranges.
  • Use pagination-sm in result bars and full size for alphabetical browse controls.
  • Wrap each direct child of bt-toolbar__left and bt-toolbar__right in bt-toolbar__item.
  • Let the bar wrap in reading order on narrow screens; do not turn it into a horizontal scroller.
  • Changing sort or page size returns to page 1.
  • Do not add mb-0 to ul.pagination; the base reset already removes its margin.
