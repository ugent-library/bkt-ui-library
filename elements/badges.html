Clickable badge

A badge that acts or navigates is a real <button> or <a> carrying .badge. It renders squared (vs the pill status badge) with hover and focus states — the shape is the affordance, not colour. Pick the look with badge--outline or a text-bg-* variant. Used for active-filter chips; the split chip joins two with .filter-chip-group (see Filter picker).

Outline clickable badge — the active-filter chip
Year: 2024

The chip whose editor is open adds Bootstrap's .active (the outline chip fills blue) and aria-current="true" on the label half. A state class on badge--outline, not a separate variant.
