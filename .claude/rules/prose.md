# Prose

Read `docs/SPEC-WRITING.md` before editing prose. Treat its word limits as ceilings.
Write only facts that change a decision, action or understanding. Use plain
subject-verb sentences and one term per concept. Delete preambles, duplicated facts,
process narration and closing summaries. Run the final cut silently; never report the
pass or enumerate routine edits.

Before deleting, classify requirements, decisions, rationale, warnings and open
questions. Preserve them in their current owner or relocate them first. A concise
source-local `Prototype note:` may remain in HTML; do not create a document merely to
mirror it.
