# [researcher][09] Lists and saved searches

## Why

Data stewards teach these first at the start-of-year Biblio intro: a list collects
works, a saved search re-runs a scoped query. Raven has both. Wim Webb (his list, on
his own site) needs a stable address. Create, save and reopen are not designed beyond
the lists pages.

## What

- [ ] Lists: create, add from a card, reopen — `lists`, `list-detail`, `list-edit` exist
- [ ] Saved searches: save from the builder (raven #229 Share and Save), reopen, re-run
- [ ] Embed: does raven's `saved_searches` support an embed, or only re-running? Ask raven
