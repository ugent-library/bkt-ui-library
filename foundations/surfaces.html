Choose a surface

Choose the surface from the product layer, not from the user, subject matter, task, authentication, vocabulary or desired density. A researcher editing their record is in the backoffice; a staff member viewing the same record on the public site is on the public surface.

A public preview inside a backoffice editor may declare a nested public surface. The editor and its controls remain backoffice.

For the layer definition and integration contract, see docs/SURFACES.md and docs/UI-LAYER.md.
